Ano ang Nutrition Month?

Nutrition Month is celebrated every July in the Philippines to promote the importance of good nutrition and healthy eating habits. It encourages every Filipino to make healthier food choices and live an active lifestyle. The celebration is led by the National Nutrition Council (NNC), which organizes different activities to educate children, families, schools, workplaces, and communities about proper nutrition and overall health.

The celebration reminds everyone that eating nutritious food is one of the best ways to prevent illnesses and maintain a healthy body. It also raises awareness about problems such as malnutrition, obesity, vitamin and mineral deficiencies, and other health conditions caused by poor eating habits.

bakit mahalaga ang Nutritiom?

Nutrition is the process of providing our bodies with the nutrients needed to grow, stay healthy, and perform daily activities. The food we eat gives us energy, strengthens our immune system, helps our brains function better, and supports the growth of muscles and bones.

Children and teenagers especially need proper nutrition because they are still growing. Eating healthy foods helps improve concentration, memory, learning ability, and physical development. Adults also benefit from good nutrition by reducing the risk of diseases such as diabetes, hypertension, heart disease, and obesity.

The Six Essential Nutrients

  • Carbohydrates – The body's main source of energy.
  • Proteins – Build and repair muscles, tissues, and cells.
  • Fats – Provide energy and protect the body's organs.
  • Vitamins – Help keep the body healthy and support different body functions.
  • Minerals – Strengthen bones, teeth, and help maintain proper body functions.
  • Water – Keeps the body hydrated and helps transport nutrients throughout the body.

Healthy Foods to Eat

A healthy diet includes a variety of foods from different food groups. Fruits and vegetables provide vitamins, minerals, and fiber. Whole grains such as brown rice, oats, and whole wheat bread supply long-lasting energy. Protein-rich foods like fish, chicken, eggs, beans, and lean meat help build and repair body tissues. Milk and dairy products provide calcium for strong bones and teeth.

Drinking enough water every day is also important. Instead of sugary drinks, choose water whenever possible. Limiting junk food, sweets, salty snacks, and highly processed foods can help prevent many health problems.

Healthy Habits During Nutrition Month

  • Eat balanced meals every day.
  • Eat plenty of fruits and vegetables.
  • Drink at least 8 glasses of water daily.
  • Exercise or stay physically active for at least 30 minutes each day.
  • Get enough sleep every night.
  • Avoid excessive consumption of junk food, soft drinks, and sweets.
  • Practice proper hygiene by washing hands before eating.

Conclusion

Nutrition Month reminds us that good health begins with proper nutrition. By making healthy food choices, staying active, drinking enough water, and maintaining a balanced lifestyle, we can improve our quality of life and reduce the risk of diseases. Everyone children, teenagers, adults, and senior citizens can benefit from healthy eating habits. Remember, a healthy body leads to a healthier, happier, and more productive future.
